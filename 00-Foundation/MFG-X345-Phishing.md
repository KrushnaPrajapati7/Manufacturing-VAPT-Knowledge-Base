# MFG-X345: Phishing

## Question

What should an authorized manufacturing VAPT auditor consider regarding **Phishing**?

## Description

Cross-cutting manufacturing security knowledge that can apply across multiple VAPT phases and asset families.

## Coverage

- Establish whether the capability/system/process exists and is in scope.
- Identify owner, business purpose, dependencies and environment.
- Identify relevant security controls and trust boundaries.
- Determine applicable assessment phase(s).
- Record evidence without unnecessarily collecting sensitive information.
- Map material observations to technical, business, operational and safety impact.
- Link to the relevant manufacturing asset and VAPT knowledge units.

## Safety

Do not perform disruptive activity against production OT, safety systems or cyber-physical equipment without explicit authorization, safety review, approved window and defined stop conditions.

## Output

Document applicability as one of:

- In scope and assessed.
- In scope but not tested.
- Not applicable.
- Out of scope.
- Unknown / requires discovery.

## References

Use the applicable NIST, ISA/IEC 62443, ISA-95, MITRE ATT&CK for ICS, OWASP or organizational standards referenced by the related knowledge unit.
