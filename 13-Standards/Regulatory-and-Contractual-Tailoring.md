# Regulatory and Contractual Tailoring

A manufacturing VAPT cannot use one universal legal checklist for every company.

For each engagement, identify:

- Country and state/jurisdiction.
- Industry subtype.
- Critical-infrastructure status if applicable.
- Personal-data obligations if applicable.
- Sector-specific obligations.
- Customer contractual security requirements.
- Supplier/vendor security clauses.
- Product-security requirements.
- Export-control or intellectual-property constraints where applicable.
- Incident-reporting obligations.
- Record-retention requirements.

For India-based engagements, the assessment team should separately determine which current Indian laws, rules, CERT-In directions, sectoral requirements, contractual requirements and customer requirements apply to the specific organization. Do not copy a generic legal list into every report without checking current applicability.

Legal/compliance interpretation should be confirmed with the organization's legal/compliance function or qualified counsel.
