# Manufacturing VAPT Report Framework

## Cover
- Client / organization
- Assessment name
- Sites/environments
- Assessment dates
- Report version
- Classification
- Prepared/reviewed/approved by

## 1. Executive Summary
Purpose, scope, overall coverage, material risk themes, key business/production considerations, limitations and remediation themes.

## 2. Engagement and Authorization
Authorization, ROE, testing window, stakeholders, third-party approvals and safety constraints.

## 3. Scope and Asset Coverage
Asset families, 406-asset master scope coverage, in/out-of-scope decisions, unknowns and exclusions.

## 4. Manufacturing Architecture Context
Enterprise/plant/cell boundaries, zones/conduits, IT/OT boundaries, cloud/remote access, critical processes and safety boundaries.

## 5. Methodology
Eight VAPT phases, knowledge-base methodology, tools, standards, threat/attack-path analysis and validation approach.

## 6. Testing Coverage
Phase coverage, asset coverage, test status, not-tested reasons and limitations.

## 7. Risk Summary
Finding counts by severity/status/category plus production, quality, safety, recovery and IP considerations.

## 8. Detailed Findings
Use the finding template for every confirmed finding.

## 9. OT/ICS and Safety Considerations
Passive/controlled testing, process constraints, safety implications, recovery considerations and validation limitations.

## 10. Business Impact
Production, quality, safety, delivery, financial, regulatory/customer, IP and recovery impact.

## 11. Remediation Plan
Priorities, owners, target dates, compensating controls and residual risk.

## 12. Retest Results
Original finding, remediation claimed, retest method, evidence, result and residual risk.

## 13. Limitations and Exceptions
Out-of-scope, not-tested, unknown, unavailable access, production restrictions and accepted risks.

## 14. Conclusion
Factual summary of assessment coverage and remaining exposure; do not overstate assurance.

## Appendices
Asset inventory, test register, evidence index, finding register, standards mapping, tools, attack paths, glossary and change log.
