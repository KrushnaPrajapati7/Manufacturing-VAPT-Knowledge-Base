# Manufacturing VAPT Auditor Operating Procedure

## Mandatory controls
- Written authorization before testing.
- Scope and ownership confirmed.
- ROE approved.
- Safety gate completed for OT/ICS.
- Production-impacting tests explicitly approved.
- Evidence classification and retention defined.
- Third-party assets require third-party authorization where applicable.
- Unknown / N/A / Out of Scope / Not Tested are valid states; do not infer coverage.

## Stop conditions
Stop and escalate for unexpected process behavior, safety-system impact, uncontrolled state changes, instability, loss of monitoring, unexpected production impact, unexpected authentication/lockout behavior, evidence-handling issues, or any condition defined in the ROE.
